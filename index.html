Hype Squad Desk

A private tool its owner uses to manage their own social media accounts in one place.

Hype Squad Desk runs on its owner's computer. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it. It:

  • publishes the owner's videos to the owner's own accounts, on the platforms that allow it;
  • reads the public statistics of those accounts and their posts (followers, views, likes, comments, shares);
  • keeps those statistics on the owner's computer, so the owner can compare how each video performs.

It follows each platform's terms and developer policies, and it doesn't collect anyone else's data.
