Privacy Policy

Hype Squad Desk · last updated 30 October 2026

Hype Squad Desk is a private tool. Its only user is its owner, who uses it to publish videos to the Facebook Pages, Instagram, Threads and TikTok accounts that the owner manages, and to read the statistics of those accounts and posts. The tool isn't offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

What the tool accesses

  • The accounts the owner has authorized: Facebook Pages, Instagram, Threads and TikTok.
  • The posts the owner publishes through it, or links to it.
  • The statistics of those accounts and posts: followers, views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, follows and watch time.

It does not collect personal data about other people. The only information it keeps about other users is the total counts on the owner's own accounts and posts.

Where data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They aren't sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and they aren't used for advertising.

Data deletion

To delete all data the tool holds:

  • Remove the app's access:
    • Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites.
    • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.
    • Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions.
    • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
  • The locally stored tokens and statistics are deleted on request.
